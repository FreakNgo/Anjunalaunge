Named for a sunset in Goa

Anjuna is a stretch of Goan coast known for its flea markets and long, unhurried evenings — the kind of place where nobody's in a rush to be anywhere else. That's the mood we've brought to Hazelwell Street.

A few minutes from Bournville station, Anjuna Lounge serves proudly vegetarian- and vegan-friendly Indian street food alongside a full bar of cocktails, beer and spirits, in a room that feels more like a beach shack than a restaurant.

Out back there's a bright, dog-friendly garden for the warmer months — bring the whole crew, four legs included.

Small plates, proper spice

Street-food-style dishes built for sharing, done properly — not watered down for anyone.

Expect small plates meant to pile up in the middle of the table, a genuinely generous vegan and vegetarian range, and a bar that doesn't treat cocktails as an afterthought. Whether you're in for a quick bite before the train or settling in for the evening, the pace here is the same: slow down.
